# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the **NetForge MCP** project a harassment-free, welcoming, and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively and politely in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Respect differing technical viewpoints and focus feedback on code, architecture, and reliability.
- Credit contributors fairly and maintain open-source licensing integrity.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, or publishing others' private information.
- Submitting deliberately malicious code, credential harvesters, or unsafe automation bypasses.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or close comments, PRs, or issues that violate this Code of Conduct. Report concerns via repository security/maintainer contacts.
